## Description
*(4,000 characters max)*

```
EcoProposal puts everything a field rep needs to close a job in one place — from the first site visit to the signed proposal in the customer's hands.

BUILD THE PROPOSAL ON-SITE
Add line items with quantities, unit prices, and descriptions. Tax and totals calculate automatically. Start from scratch or duplicate a previous job to save time.

CAPTURE THE JOB WITH PHOTOS
Take photos with your camera or pull from your library and attach them directly to the proposal. Annotate any photo with arrows, circles, and text to highlight exactly what the customer is getting.

SKETCH IT OUT
Use the built-in drawing canvas to sketch site layouts, room diagrams, or installation plans on a clean grid or compass-rose background. Save multiple sketch pages per proposal.

MEASURE AND RECORD TAKEOFFS
Log field measurements in the takeoff section and keep them with the rest of the job — no separate spreadsheet needed.

COLLECT A SIGNATURE
Present the finished proposal and capture the customer's signature on-screen before you leave the job site.

PROFESSIONAL PDF IN SECONDS
Generate a branded Eco Seal PDF with your company information, customer details, line items, photos, sketches, and signature. Preview it on-screen or share it directly via email.

SAVE TO ONEDRIVE
Sign in with your Eco Seal Microsoft 365 account and proposals save automatically to your OneDrive — accessible from any device and shareable with the office.

BUILT FOR ECO SEAL REPS
EcoProposal is an internal tool for Eco Seal Home Solutions. Sign-in requires an Eco Seal company account.
```

## What's New (Version 1.1)
*(Release notes shown in App Store and TestFlight — keep it short)*

```
NEW: INSPECTION REPORTS
Build out a complete Service Report — Jobsite Condition Assessment right on your iPad. Switch to the new Inspections tab in the sidebar and walk the GMX warranty checklist top to bottom: job and warranty info, who was on site, reason for the visit, all 20 jobsite-condition checks (OK / Issue / N/A), flood and humidistat testing, and your findings with the warranty determination.

• Capture site photos with the camera or photo library, add captions, and mark them up with Apple Pencil — they're added to the report PDF automatically, one per page.
• Collect technician and homeowner acknowledgement signatures on-screen.
• Generate a clean, two-page branded PDF that matches the paper form, ready to share by email.

NEW: SERVICE DIVISION
Proposals now support a third line of business. Create a Service proposal and check off the work performed — sump pump repair or replacement, battery backup, annual maintenance, drain cleaning, crack repair, dehumidifier service, warranty visits, and more. The service checklist prints on the contract PDF just like Remodel scope does.
```
